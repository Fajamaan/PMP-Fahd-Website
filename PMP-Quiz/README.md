# Pass Your PMP — Practice Quiz

A self-contained web app built from your `pass-your-pmp-stdnt.pptx` deck (228 practice questions).

## How to use
Just **double-click `index.html`** — it opens in your browser. No server, no internet needed to take the quiz.

### What it does
- Shows one question at a time (228 total), with the original slide number.
- Click an answer → it instantly:
  - highlights the **correct** answer in green (✓),
  - marks your choice red (✗) if it was wrong,
  - shows a short **explanation**.
- **"Select two/three" questions**: tick your choices, then press **Check answer**.
- **✨ Analyze with Claude** button → opens a Claude chat pre-filled with the question, the
  correct answer, and your choice, asking Claude to explain *why* the answer is right, why your
  pick was right/wrong, and why the other options are wrong.
- **🌐 العربية** toggle shows the original Arabic translation of each question.
- Tracks your score, supports Previous/Next, ← → arrow keys, and "Go to" a question number.

## Notes
- The answer key was compiled for study practice. If any answer looks off, click **Analyze with
  Claude** — that live analysis is the authoritative explanation.
- `index.html` is the only file you need; everything is embedded in it.
